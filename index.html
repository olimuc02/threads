Threads

A calmer way to plan what you buy

Threads is a personal clothing & thrift wishlist. Group what you want into categories like “Denim Jeans,” save links and prices, set a budget goal for each item, and check things off as you get them.

🗂️ Organized categories 🎯 Per-item budget goals ⭐ Priorities 📌 Pinterest quick-add

Threads runs privately on your own device. The optional Pinterest connection only reads your own boards and pins so you can import them into your list.
