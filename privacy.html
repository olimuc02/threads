Privacy Policy

Threads Clothing Wishlist · Last updated May 2026

Threads is a personal clothing and thrift wishlist application. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In plain terms: Threads is built for one person's own use and keeps your data on your own device.

What we store

  • Your wishlist — categories, items, links, prices, budget goals, and priorities — is saved locally in your own web browser on your own device. It is not uploaded to any server we run.
  • Threads has no user accounts, no analytics, and no advertising.

Pinterest connection (optional)

If you choose to connect Pinterest, Threads uses Pinterest's official API only to:

  • read the names of your own boards, and
  • read the pins on a board you select (their title, link, and image),

so that you can import them into your wishlist. Threads requests read-only access (boards:read, pins:read). It does not create, edit, or delete anything on your Pinterest account, and it does not access anyone else's account. Your Pinterest access token is held only on your own device for the duration of your session and is never shared with third parties.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent, or share your data.
  • We don't track you across other sites.
  • We don't send your wishlist or Pinterest data to any external service.

Data removal

Because your data lives in your browser, you can remove it at any time by clearing the app's data in your browser, or by disconnecting Pinterest from within your Pinterest account settings.
